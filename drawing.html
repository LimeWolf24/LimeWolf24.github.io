I chose Sakura Mochi for my food as I like to incorperate anime into my art, and with this project having to be food, I decided I was going to look for the different foods my favorite anime characters like, I decided with me doing a lot of My Hero Academia things in Middle school, and me having recently finished Demon Slayer, I decided I wanted to do something different and I searched for Tanjiro, Inosuke, Sanemi, Obanai, and finally Mitsuri. I liked the one for Mitsuri as I had remembered in the anime, the episode of her back story, she was born with black hair, very strong, and a very strong appitie, and with that being said, her liking of Sakura Mochi, she ate so much of it, her hair turned pink with green ends. I found that very fitting for this drawing as it is something interesting, and it is food.
